Placement at the trailing edge of the bottom toolbar.

## Overview

The `.toolbarBottomTralling` case puts the inset view at the trailing edge of the bottom toolbar. This is a common spot for primary actions at the bottom of the screen.

> Important: This placement is primarily for **watchOS**. While available on iOS for compatibility, use other placements for standard iOS layouts.

### Availability

This placement works on **iOS** and **watchOS**.

### Use Case

Ideal for a primary confirmation button or a "Next" action at the bottom-right of the interface.
